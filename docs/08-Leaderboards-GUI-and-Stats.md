# 🏆 08. 54-Slot Leaderboards GUI (`/pinata top`)

PinataSpectra features persistent multi-category statistics tracking and an interactive 54-slot GUI menu accessible with `/pinata top`.

---

## 📊 Ranking Categories

The `/pinata top` GUI ranks players across three persistent leaderboards:
1. 💥 **Top Damage Dealers:** Total cumulative damage dealt to all piñatas.
2. 👑 **Top MVPs / Slayers:** Total piñatas destroyed (last-hit slayer count).
3. 🍬 **Top Candies Consumed:** Total tactical event candies consumed during combat.

---

## 🖥️ GUI Features

* **Animated Glass Panes:** Shifting rainbow borders on the menu perimeter.
* **Custom Player Skulls:** Slots #1 through #10 display the exact player skin skull with detailed stats and rank in the item lore.
* **Asynchronous Data Loading:** Database queries are cached and fetched in background threads, eliminating UI lag.
